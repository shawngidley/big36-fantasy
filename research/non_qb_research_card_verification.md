# Non-QB Research Card Verification

The local Research page was checked after the certified non-QB stat overlay was applied. The RB tab rendered 136 cards with the updated official first-12-game badges, including touchdowns and fumbles lost. Each visible RB card carried the disclosure that the stat line is certified from CFBD player and team boxes while historical tiered points remain held pending complete touchdown-distance and event-ownership validation.

The displayed point totals were retained without re-tiering. This separation is intentional: it prevents an unverified historical play-distance calculation from being presented as a certified point total.
