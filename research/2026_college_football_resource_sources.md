# 2026 College Football Resource Hub Sources

External resources verified for the public 36 Football research hub on August 21, 2026. These links are intended as owner-facing research aids; they do not replace the league's internal scoring ledger.

## Curation Notes

Use at least two sources when making a major draft decision. Depth-chart pages can lag injury reports and coach announcements; recruiting and transfer pages change throughout the cycle; ratings and projections are context rather than a guarantee of on-field production.
