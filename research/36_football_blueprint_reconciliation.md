# 36 Football Inaugural Blueprint Reconciliation

## Confirmed current requirements

The uploaded 36 Football blueprint supersedes the earlier Big 36 pilot specification wherever the two differ. The inaugural league contains 36 owner-created programs in six conferences, with six selections per program: **QB, RB, WR, TE, K/ST, and DEF**. A school may be selected at multiple positions, but a school-position group can be drafted only once.

The point model is now implemented in the shared contracts. Offensive touchdowns score 6, 8, 10, or 12 by the 1–9, 10–29, 30–59, and 60+ snap-distance tiers. Passing TDs and successful passing conversions credit both the QB and the scoring offensive position; rushing scores credit only the scorer’s official preseason position. Offensive turnovers deduct three points. K/ST and DEF are separate groups with the detailed block, safety, return, turnover, sack, and shutout values set out in the blueprint.

## Live-data capacity

CollegeFootballData Tier 4 is the correct live subscription for the complete blueprint. Tier 1 grants access to live endpoints, but its 5,000 monthly request cap is insufficient for a one-minute update cadence across a full 136-school pool. Tier 4 provides 125,000 requests per month, giving practical headroom for scoreboard polling, active-game plays, roster refreshes, correction checks, and postgame reconciliation.

## Confirmed live-scoring safeguards

The automatic worker will use the provider’s official source attribution, roster-position designation, and stable play IDs. It will store each derived score event with a source key, update provisional values in real time, reverse or replace events after provider corrections, and reconcile final games. Every public score change will have a corresponding live-feed/audit explanation.
